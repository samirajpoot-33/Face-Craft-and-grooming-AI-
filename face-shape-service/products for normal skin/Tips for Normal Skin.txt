Normal skin is well-balanced, with minimal sensitivity and a healthy glow. Maintaining this balance is key.

Daily Gentle Cleanser – Use a mild, sulfate-free cleanser to remove dirt without stripping natural oils.

Skin Repair Serum – A lightweight serum with niacinamide or hyaluronic acid helps maintain hydration and repair the skin barrier.

Moisturizer – Apply a balanced moisturizer to keep skin smooth and prevent moisture loss.

Sun Protection – Use a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily to protect against UV damage and premature aging.

Consistency is Key – Stick to a simple routine and avoid over-exfoliating to keep your skin healthy long-term.
