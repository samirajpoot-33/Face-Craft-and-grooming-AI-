Oily Skin

Oily skin produces excess sebum, which can lead to shine, clogged pores, and acne if not managed properly.

Oil-Control Cleanser – Use a gel or foaming cleanser to remove excess oil without over-drying the skin.

Balancing Serum – Apply a serum with niacinamide or salicylic acid to control oil, reduce pores, and prevent breakouts.

Lightweight Moisturizer – Choose an oil-free, non-comedogenic moisturizer to keep skin hydrated without clogging pores.

Matte Sunscreen – Use a lightweight, gel-based SPF 30+ sunscreen designed for oily or acne-prone skin.
