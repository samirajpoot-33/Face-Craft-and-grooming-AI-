Dry Skin

Dry skin lacks moisture and may feel tight, rough, or flaky. Hydration and repair are essential.

Hydrating Cleanser – Choose a cream-based or hydrating cleanser to cleanse without drying out the skin.

Repair & Hydration Serum – Use serums with hyaluronic acid, ceramides, or peptides to restore moisture and strengthen the skin barrier.

Rich Moisturizer – Apply a deeply nourishing moisturizer to lock in hydration and reduce dryness.

Daily Sunscreen – Opt for a moisturizing SPF 30+ sunscreen to protect sensitive, dry skin from sun damage.
